### Key Factors Affecting Resale

- **New model launches** cause the sharpest drops (especially when a new chip generation arrives)
- **Higher-spec configs** (more RAM, storage, Ultra chips) retain value better in absolute terms but lose more in percentage
- **Selling at 2–3 years** is the sweet spot — you get good use while the machine still fetches a reasonable price
- **Current market data:** M2 Ultra Mac Studios (orig. $3,999–$7,999) are selling for ~$1,800–$2,750 used (45–55% depreciation after ~2–3 years)

## Part 2: Indian Income Tax Depreciation Benefits

### Applicable Rules

- **Section 32** of the Income Tax Act, 1961
- **Asset Class:** Computers (under Plant & Machinery)
- **Depreciation Rate:** **40% per annum** on Written Down Value (WDV)
- **Method:** Written Down Value (WDV) — not straight line
- **Additional Depreciation (Sec 32(1)(iia)):** **NOT available** for computers (classified as "office appliances")

### Important Rules & Conditions

1. **180-Day Rule:** If the asset is used for less than 180 days in the year of purchase, only **50% of the year's depreciation** (i.e., 20% instead of 40%) is allowed. The balance is added to the next year.

2. **Business Use Required:** The Mac Studios must be used for business or professional purposes. Personal use is not eligible.

3. **Block of Assets:** All computers are grouped into one "block." When you sell one, the sale proceeds reduce the block's WDV rather than creating a separate capital gain (unless the entire block is sold).

4. **No Additional Depreciation:** Computers are explicitly excluded from the 20% additional depreciation under Section 32(1)(iia) as they are classified as office appliances.

5. **GST Input Credit:** If registered under GST, you can also claim Input Tax Credit on the GST paid on purchase — this is separate from income tax depreciation.

## Part 3: Side-by-Side Comparison

| Year | Book Value (Tax WDV) | Realistic Resale | Gap (Resale > Book) |
|------|----------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| 1    | ₹19,92,000 ($24,000) | $30,000          | +$6,000 (resale higher) |
| 2    | ₹11,95,200 ($14,400) | $23,000          | +$8,600 (resale higher) |
| 3    | ₹7,17,120 ($8,640)   | $17,000          | +$8,360 (resale higher) |
| 4    | ₹4,30,272 ($5,184)   | $13,000          | +$7,816 (resale higher) |
| 5    | ₹2,58,163 ($3,110)   | $9,000           | +$5,890 (resale higher) |

**Key insight:** The 40% WDV depreciation for tax purposes is **much faster** than actual
market depreciation. This means:

- You get **accelerated tax benefits** in the early years
- If you sell the machines, the sale price will likely exceed the WDV, creating a
  **taxable short-term capital gain** (added back to the block of assets or taxed as
  business income if the block ceases to exist)
- **Optimal strategy:** Hold for 3+ years to maximize the tax depreciation benefit
  before selling. By year 3, the tax WDV drops to ~$8,640 while resale is still ~$17,000
